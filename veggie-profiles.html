🥕 Carrot – Veggie Profile

  • Growing Season:
    Best grown in spring and autumn.
    Prefers cooler temperatures (10–21°C).
    Avoid hot summer planting—carrots can become bitter or bolt.
  • Growing Difficulty:
    ✅ Easy to grow – great for beginners!
    Requires loose, well-drained soil for straight root growth.
  • Time to Harvest:
    Usually ready in 60–80 days from sowing.
  • Sunlight Needs:
    Grows best in full sun, but can tolerate partial shade.
  • Fun Fact:
    Carrots come in more than just orange—look out for purple, red, yellow, and white varieties too!
    They're rich in beta-carotene, which is great for eye health.

🌽 Corn – Veggie Profile

  • Growing Season:
    Thrives in late spring through summer.
    Requires warm temperatures (ideally above 18°C) and plenty of sunshine.
    Frost-sensitive – make sure all risk of frost has passed before planting.
  • Growing Difficulty:
    ✅ Moderate to easy – best with some space and care.
    Grows best in well-drained, fertile soil with regular watering.
    Benefits from being planted in blocks rather than single rows for better pollination.
  • Time to Harvest:
    Usually ready in 70–100 days from sowing, depending on the variety.
  • Sunlight Needs:
    Requires full sun – at least 6–8 hours of direct sunlight per day.

🥦 Broccoli – Veggie Profile

  • Growing Season:
    Best grown in cool seasons—spring and autumn.
    Thrives in temperatures between 15–20°C.
    Avoid hot weather as it can cause bolting or small heads.
  • Growing Difficulty:
    ✅ Moderate – requires a bit of care.
    Needs nutrient-rich soil and regular watering.
    Protect from pests like caterpillars and aphids.
  • Time to Harvest:
    Usually ready in 60–100 days from transplanting.
    Harvest the main head before flowers open; side shoots can follow.
  • Sunlight Needs:
    Grows best in full sun – at least 6 hours of direct sunlight daily.

🍅 Tomato – Veggie Profile

  • Growing Season:
    Best grown in warm seasons—spring and summer.
    Prefers temperatures between 18–25°C.
    Avoid frost and chilly weather, as it can damage the plants.
  • Growing Difficulty:
    ✅ Moderate – requires a bit of attention.
    Needs well-drained, fertile soil and regular watering.
    Be mindful of pests like aphids and diseases like blight.
  • Time to Harvest:
    Typically ready in 55–85 days from planting, depending on variety.
    Harvest when the tomatoes are fully ripe and have a deep color.
  • Sunlight Needs:
    Grows best in full sun – at least 6-8 hours of direct sunlight daily.

🥬 Cabbage – Veggie Profile

  • Growing Season:
    Best grown in cooler seasons—spring and autumn.
    Prefers temperatures between 15–20°C.
    Avoid hot weather, as cabbage can become bitter in high heat.
  • Growing Difficulty:
    ✅ Easy to grow – great for beginners!
    Requires fertile, well-drained soil with good moisture retention.
    Keep an eye out for pests like cabbage worms and diseases like clubroot.
  • Time to Harvest:
    Usually ready in 70–100 days from sowing, depending on variety and conditions.
    Harvest when the heads are firm and the outer leaves are tight.
  • Sunlight Needs:
    Grows best in full sun – at least 6 hours of direct sunlight daily.

🥔 Potato – Veggie Profile

  • Growing Season:
    Best grown in cool to mild temperatures (10–20°C).
    Ideal for spring planting to ensure a summer harvest.
  • Growing Difficulty:
    ✅ Moderately easy to grow – great for intermediate gardeners.
    Requires loose, well-drained soil with a lot of organic matter.
    Keep an eye on pests like aphids and potato beetles.
  • Time to Harvest:
    Usually ready in 70–120 days from planting, depending on variety and growing conditions.
    Harvest when the leaves start to die back, and the skins are firm.
  • Sunlight Needs:
    Grows best in full sun – at least 6 hours of direct sunlight daily.
